About FuelSHOT

FuelSHOT is an innovative on-demand emergency fuel delivery service that aims to revolutionize how people refuel in urgent situations. Whether your vehicle breaks down due to an empty tank, or you're in a remote area without access to a fuel station—FuelSHOT ensures quick and reliable delivery of fuel right to your location.

Our mission is simple: To eliminate fuel emergencies by making petrol and diesel instantly accessible, anywhere, anytime. We understand the frustration and inconvenience of running out of fuel, especially in critical situations. That's why we've created a service that brings the fuel station to you.

With our network of certified fuel suppliers and delivery vehicles, we guarantee high-quality fuel delivered safely and efficiently. Our user-friendly platform allows you to place orders in just a few clicks, with real-time tracking so you know exactly when to expect your delivery.

At FuelSHOT, we're committed to providing exceptional service, reliability, and peace of mind to all our customers. Join thousands of satisfied customers who have made FuelSHOT their trusted fuel delivery partner.

Rescue Fuel Service

Our Rescue Fuel service is designed for emergency situations when you find yourself stranded with an empty tank. This premium service prioritizes your delivery to get you back on the road as quickly as possible.

What Our Rescue Service Includes:

  • Priority delivery within 20 minutes (in service areas)
  • 24/7 availability including holidays
  • Real-time tracking of your delivery
